Domina el Present Simple con Idíoslinguas

El Presente Simple se utiliza para hablar de rutinas, hábitos, hechos generales y estados permanentes. Aprende la regla de la tercera persona, conjugaciones afirmativas, negativas y preguntas.

+

Oraciones Afirmativas

Sujeto + Verbo (base / -s / -es)

I / You / We / They:

"I work every day."

(Yo trabajo todos los días)

He / She / It (3ra Persona):

"She works every day."

(Ella trabaja todos los días)

¡Atención! En afirmativo, solo añadimos -s / -es cuando el sujeto es He, She o It.
-

Oraciones Negativas

Sujeto + don't/doesn't + Verbo Base

I / You / We / They + DON'T:

"They don't like coffee."

(A ellos no les gusta el café)

He / She / It + DOESN'T:

"He doesn't like coffee."

(A él no le gusta el café)

Regla de oro: Al usar doesn't, el verbo vuelve a su forma base (¡SIN 'S'!).
?

Preguntas e Interrogación

Do / Does + Sujeto + Verbo Base ?

DO + (I, you, we, they):

"Do you speak English?"

(¿Hablas tú inglés?)

DOES + (he, she, it):

"Does she live in Spain?"

(¿Vive ella en España?)

Respuestas cortas: Yes, I do / No, I don't. Yes, she does / No, she doesn't.

Grammar Matrix
Regla General + S

A la mayoría de los verbos se les agrega simplemente '-s'.

  • play → plays
  • work → works
  • eat → eats
Terminados en -o, -ch, -sh, -ss, -x + ES

Si termina en sonido sibilante o vocal 'o', agregamos '-es'.

  • watch → watches
  • wash → washes
  • go → goes
Consonante + Y -y → + IES

Cambiamos la 'y' por 'i' y agregamos '-es'.

  • study → studies
  • fly → flies
  • try → tries

A1 Reading Comprehension & Glossary

A Day in the Life of Sarah

Lee la historia en inglés adaptada para nivel principiante (A1). Explora el glosario interactivo de vocabulario y responde las 10 preguntas de comprensión lectora.

Reading Text (Level A1)
Present Simple in Context

Sarah is 28 years old and she works as a wildlife photographer in Kenya. She loves animals and nature. Every morning, Sarah gets up at 5:30 AM. She drinks a cup of hot tea and eats fruit for breakfast.

At 6:15 AM, she packs her camera equipment, gets into her green jeep, and drives into the savannah before sunrise. Wild animals, like lions, elephants, and giraffes, are very active early in the morning. Sarah takes hundreds of beautiful photos every day.

She does not return to her camp until 6:00 PM. In the evening, she edits her photographs on her laptop and chats with her team. She goes to bed early because she starts work early again the next day. Sarah is very happy with her job!
